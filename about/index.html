About Makino

From a 1990 foundation to a friction-technology brand trusted across vehicle categories.

The Makino story is one of manufacturing depth, R&D investment, and the discipline required to support both large OEMs and demanding aftermarket distributors.
Timeline
1990

Technical collaboration begins

Makino starts with friction-disc expertise and lays the foundation for long-term clutch and brake specialization.

1992

OEM supply expands

Supplies to Escorts Yamaha, Bajaj, and Piaggio help establish the early manufacturing and quality reputation.

1999

4W brake and disc pad development

The company broadens its portfolio and deepens engineering capability across more demanding applications.

2006

DSIR-approved R&D center

A major signal of technical depth and internal capability for product development, validation, and improvement.

2012

Japanese technology tie-up

Advanced collaboration reinforces process quality, technology access, and future-facing manufacturing know-how.

2021

Manufacturing excellence recognition

JIPM-linked and TPM excellence milestones underline Makino's operational maturity and production discipline.
